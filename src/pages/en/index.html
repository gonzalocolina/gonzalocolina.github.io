How I work

  1. Measure before claiming

    In Optiline a new network is only promoted if it wins a full-game SPRT; a lower validation loss is not enough. That is why every number here comes with its method.

  2. Finish and ship

    Aquilae has been used in three schools, Pasos de María is on Google Play and Optiline publishes releases with Linux and Windows binaries.

  3. Report what did not work

    The case studies include the weak results: the rare signs RoadSignAI misses, the newer model that did not improve, the test runs that produced false draws.
