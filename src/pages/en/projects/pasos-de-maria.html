Pasos de María

An educational app about Queen María de Molina and the places in Valladolid linked to her, for children and adults. It won 3rd prize at the OnAppExperience contest and is on Google Play in Spanish, English, French and German.

Problem

Bring the story of María de Molina, Queen of Castile, and the places in Valladolid linked to her to very different audiences: adults, children and school groups with their teacher.

Features

  • Interactive map of the points of interest, with GPS navigation to each place.
  • 360° panoramic photos of the monuments.
  • A quiz, solo or real-time multiplayer across devices, with a teacher mode for running games in class.
  • A children’s mode where María de Molina tells her own story.
  • Light and dark themes, tablet and orientation support, and full translation into 4 languages.

Architecture

  1. UIJetpack Compose · Material 3
  2. ViewModelsMVVM · Hilt
  3. Repositorieslocal and remote data
  4. FirebaseAuth · real-time Firestore
  • Multiplayer with Firestore listeners. Games sync in real time across devices without a custom server.
  • MVVM with Hilt: the UI talks to ViewModels, which talk to injected repositories.
  • Crashlytics to record crashes on users’ devices.
  • OpenStreetMap for maps.
